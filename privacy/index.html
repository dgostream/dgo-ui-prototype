2. Personal information we collect and where it comes from

The information we process depends on how you access DGO, which features you use, your country, the purchase channel, and the permissions you choose.

Information you provide

  • •Account and contact information: name, email address, phone number, account identifiers, password or authentication information, and other details used to register or recover an account.
  • •Profile and preference information: selected language, profile settings, watchlists, favourites, ratings or feedback where these features are available.
  • •Support and participation information: messages to support, complaints, survey responses, competition entries and information you give us when participating in an offer.

Information generated when you use DGO

  • •Viewing and interaction information: titles viewed, viewing progress, search queries, playback events, subscription entitlements, app interactions and use of platform features.
  • •Device and technical information: device type and model, operating system, app or browser version, device or app identifiers, IP address, network and connection diagnostics, error reports, session events, and security logs.
  • •Approximate location and rights information: the country or region inferred from network information and other signals used to apply content licensing, pricing, security and access rules. We do not describe this as precise GPS location unless a specific feature requests and uses it.
  • •Communications and measurement information: whether service messages are delivered or opened and, where enabled and permitted, data produced by analytics, cookies, software development kits (SDKs) or advertising measurements.

Information from others

  • •Payment providers and app stores may provide payment status, transaction references, subscription identifiers, billing currency, tax details, limited payment-method information, refund outcomes and fraud or payment-failure signals.
  • •Distribution, telecommunications, promotional or content partners may supply limited account-matching, offer-redemption or entitlement information when you use a partner offer.
  • •Authentication, technical, analytics or advertising providers may supply information needed to operate their integrations, subject to your choices and applicable law.

Where information is required to create an account, authenticate you, fulfil a purchase or deliver a licensed stream, we will indicate that requirement in the relevant flow. You may choose not to provide optional information, although some features may then be unavailable.

4. Accounts, authentication, subscriptions and payments

We use account identifiers and contact information to register and authenticate users, deliver one-time passwords or other login messages, recover accounts, maintain subscription status and apply device and stream limits. We may retain evidence of purchase, plan changes, cancellation status and customer authorisations when needed for billing accuracy or legal compliance.

Depending on the country and purchase channel, payments may be handled by providers including Stripe for eligible direct international purchases and eSewa, Khalti, ConnectIPS, Fonepay or GetPay for eligible Nepal purchases. Purchases billed by Apple, Google or another authorised app store are processed through that store. Available methods depend on the service and checkout presented to you.

The relevant payment provider may collect card, wallet or bank details directly under its own privacy terms. DGO may receive transaction identifiers, limited payment-method information, currency, payment status, billing country, refund or chargeback data and information needed for support and financial reconciliation. We do not ask you to send full card numbers, security codes or passwords to customer support.

Your subscription contract and cancellation rights are explained separately in the applicable subscription terms and checkout. Cancelling future renewal through DGO’s subscription-management interface does not, by itself, request deletion of your personal information or your account.

5. Devices, viewing activity and content availability

To enable streaming, DGO processes session, device, playback and entitlement information. This may include stream start and stop times, playback failures, viewing progress, technical quality, device compatibility and concurrent-session activity. We use such information to deliver the requested service, troubleshoot errors, prevent account misuse, and apply relevant content rights and plan restrictions.

Content access may be limited by country or region. We may estimate your location using IP addresses and related network signals to decide whether a title can be displayed or played and whether a particular offer is eligible. A location inferred this way may not be exact. Where a particular feature requires more precise location information, we will provide additional information or request permission as required.

Viewing history and stated preferences may support features such as Continue Watching, watchlists and recommendations. We may use aggregated or de-identified viewing statistics to assess the performance of content and the platform. We do not treat de-identified data as anonymous unless individuals cannot reasonably be identified from it.

Automated technical rules may be used to verify eligibility, detect unusual account activity or manage content rights. If we introduce decisions that require special disclosure or review rights under applicable law, we will provide those details and safeguards.

Links to external websites and services are subject to those third parties’ own policies. DGO remains responsible for its own processing and for legal obligations that apply to its use of service providers.

7. International access and data transfers

Because DGO provides services across territories, personal information may be stored, supported or accessed in Nepal, the United Kingdom and other countries where DGO’s approved service providers or associated entities operate. Applicable protections may differ by country.

Where a cross-border transfer is subject to specific legal restrictions, we assess and apply a lawful transfer mechanism, which may include a recognised adequacy arrangement, approved contractual safeguards or another permitted legal basis. We also consider appropriate security and access controls. You may request information about the transfer safeguards applicable to your data using the privacy contact in section 1.

9. Your privacy rights and choices

Depending on where you live and the applicable privacy laws, you may have the right to request access to personal data we hold about you, correction of inaccurate information, deletion, restriction of processing, portability, and objection to particular processing. You may also have rights relating to certain solely automated decisions.

Where processing relies on consent, you can withdraw that consent at any time without affecting the lawfulness of processing already carried out. You may object to direct marketing at any time; we will stop marketing to you as required by law. Where processing relies on legitimate interests, you may object and we will consider the objection under the applicable rules.

Some data can be updated directly in your DGO account. For other requests, email compliance@dishhome.com.np with your account identifier and the right you wish to exercise. We may ask for information reasonably necessary to verify your identity or authority to act for someone else.

Do not include payment-card credentials or passwords. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law and explain any lawful refusal, limitation or extension.

You may also manage permissions through relevant browser, operating-system or app settings. Those settings may not control all processing, and we will provide additional rights channels where required.

10. Children and household viewing

DGO paid accounts and subscription purchases are intended to be managed by adults who are legally able to enter into the contract. Children may watch age-appropriate content through a parent- or guardian-managed household account where permitted by the service and subject to appropriate supervision. We do not knowingly invite children to create independent paid accounts.

If we become aware that personal information relating to a child has been collected contrary to applicable law or our account rules, we will take appropriate steps, which may include restricting the account, obtaining required parental authorisation or deleting information. A parent or guardian may contact our privacy team with relevant concerns. Features likely to be accessed by children will be assessed against applicable child-privacy and age-appropriate-design obligations.

11. Information security and incidents

We maintain organisational and technical measures designed to protect personal information from unauthorised access, loss, misuse and alteration. Depending on the system, these measures may include access controls, authentication, secure transmission, logging, monitoring, vendor oversight and security reviews.

No service can guarantee absolute security. If an incident involving personal information occurs, we will assess it and notify affected individuals and relevant authorities where required by law. Please report suspected account misuse or security concerns to support@watchdgo.com or the privacy contact in section 1.

12. Privacy questions, complaints and regulators

For questions about this policy, requests to exercise rights or complaints about personal-information handling, contact compliance@dishhome.com.np. Please describe the issue, provide the account email or user identifier and explain the resolution you seek. We will investigate fairly, keep relevant records and explain the outcome. Where UK data-protection complaint rules apply, we will acknowledge receipt within the legally required period, currently 30 days, and investigate without unjustifiable delay.

You may also complain to the data-protection authority competent for your location or the relevant controller, including the UK Information Commissioner’s Office where applicable. Nothing in this policy limits a statutory right to contact a regulator or seek a legal remedy.

13. Updates to this policy

We may change this policy to reflect new services, technologies, data practices or legal requirements. The revised policy will state its effective date and be available through DGO. If a material change requires additional notice or consent under applicable law, we will give that notice or request consent before the change takes effect. Continued use of DGO does not replace a consent that law requires us to obtain.

The version applicable to a particular processing activity is the version in force when that processing occurs, subject to any additional rights or notices required by law.
